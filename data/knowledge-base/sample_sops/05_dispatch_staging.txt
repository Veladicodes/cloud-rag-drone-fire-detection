STAGING AND DISPATCH COORDINATION (ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT AN OFFICIAL SOP)

Select a staging area that is upwind of the incident, on or beside a road that
will not be cut by fire spread, with room to turn engines and land a helicopter
if needed. A point roughly 1 km upwind of the incident on the nearest through
road is a reasonable default; move it if the wind sector shifts toward it.

Dispatch sequence for an escalating detection:
- First: nearest engine or crew to the anchor point, plus a lookout to a vantage.
- Second: additional ground resources staged, not committed, until size-up
  confirms where they hold.
- Air support requested early when the head is in heavy fuel or terrain blocks
  ground access; give the aircraft the incident coordinates, wind, and a
  described anchor.

Report to dispatch on a fixed schedule: current size, percent contained,
resources committed versus staged, projected spread for the next operational
period, and any change to the evacuation arc. Pass the incident coordinates and
the staging coordinates in every escalation message so inbound resources route
to the correct point.

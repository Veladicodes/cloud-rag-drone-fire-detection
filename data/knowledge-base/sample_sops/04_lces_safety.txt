LCES SAFETY CHECK (ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT AN OFFICIAL SOP)

Before any crew engages, confirm LCES is in place and stays in place:

Lookouts — someone with a clear view of the fire and the crew, briefed on trigger
points that require the crew to disengage.

Communications — a confirmed radio link between lookout, crew, and dispatch;
a check call schedule; a backup channel.

Escape routes — at least two, timed on foot under load, kept clear and re-timed as
the fire and daylight change. If an escape route is compromised, stop work and
relocate before continuing.

Safety zones — an area large enough to survive a burn-over without a shelter,
sized to the flame length it must defend against; larger for taller fuels and
higher wind. A road, large rock scree, or the black of a completed burnout can
serve.

Re-brief the whole crew on any of: wind shift greater than about 30 degrees, a
step up in wind speed, spotting across the line, or loss of a lookout or comms
link. When in doubt, disengage to the safety zone and reassess.

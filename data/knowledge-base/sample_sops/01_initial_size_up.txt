INITIAL SIZE-UP (ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT AN OFFICIAL SOP)

On confirmation of a smoke or flame detection, the first task is a structured
size-up before any resources are committed.

Record the following within the first minutes:
- Location as latitude/longitude plus nearest access road or trail.
- Current fire size estimate and the fuel type carrying the fire (grass, brush,
  timber litter, or timber crown).
- Wind speed and direction at the incident, and any terrain that will channel it
  (canyons, saddles, chimneys).
- Values at risk downwind and downslope: structures, powerlines, camps, roads.
- Time of detection and expected time to first resource arrival.

Fire spread generally increases with wind speed, with slope, and as fuel
moisture drops. Under high wind or on steep slope, expect the head to move faster
than crews can travel; plan from the flanks, not the head.

Escalate to a larger response immediately if any of these are true: sustained
wind above roughly 25 km/h, fire established in crown fuels, spotting observed
ahead of the main fire, or an evacuation-relevant value within one hour of the
projected head.

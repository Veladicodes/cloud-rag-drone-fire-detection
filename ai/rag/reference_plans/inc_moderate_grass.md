Evacuation
- Orient the evacuation arc downwind along bearing 315 deg (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).
- Wind 14 km/h on likely grass fuel: base radius about 1.5 km with only a small addition for wind, so roughly 2 km downwind; widen if the wind direction is variable (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).
- Notify occupied structures and single-road communities inside the arc first, then routes fire could cross (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).

Containment
- Anchor on a road, stream, rock or old burn and work the flanks toward the head (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).
- Light grass at moderate wind: a narrow scratch line may hold; widen where fuel is heavier and remove overhanging fuel (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).
- Keep one flank tied to a firm barrier at all times (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).

Dispatch / staging
- Stage upwind on a road that will not be cut, about 1 km upwind of the incident (05_dispatch_staging.txt).
- First crew to the anchor plus a lookout; hold further resources staged until size-up confirms holding points (05_dispatch_staging.txt).
- Report to dispatch on a fixed schedule with size, percent contained, resources committed vs staged, and projected spread (05_dispatch_staging.txt).

Crew safety
- Confirm LCES before engaging: lookouts, confirmed comms with a check-call schedule, two timed escape routes, a safety zone sized to fuel and flame length (04_lces_safety.txt).
- Re-brief on wind shift over ~30 deg, a step up in wind, spotting, or loss of a lookout or comms (04_lces_safety.txt).

Size-up note
- Detection confidence is low (0.63): verify the detection and record location, size, fuel type carrying the fire, wind at the incident, and values at risk within the first minutes before committing resources (01_initial_size_up.txt).

Evacuation
- Orient the evacuation arc downwind along bearing 260 deg (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).
- Base radius ~1.5 km plus ~1 km per 20 km/h of wind: at 32 km/h that is about 3.1 km downwind, extended further where continuous crown fuel or upslope terrain lies in the downwind sector (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).
- Widen the arc angle because a strong wind can carry a frontal shift; re-issue on any shift over ~30 deg (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).
- Notify occupied structures, campgrounds and single-road communities in the arc, then threatened routes (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).

Containment
- Do not build line at or ahead of the head under this spread; anchor and work the cooler flank, holding the downwind head for last (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).
- High fuel dryness and wind well above ~20 km/h: use the widest line resources allow, back it with a burnout where safe, post holding crews (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).
- If a flank cannot be held, fall back to the next barrier rather than leaving an open line (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).

Dispatch / staging
- Stage upwind on a through road that will not be cut, about 1 km upwind; relocate if the wind sector shifts toward staging (05_dispatch_staging.txt).
- Commit the first resource to the anchor plus a lookout; keep other resources staged, not committed, until size-up confirms holding points (05_dispatch_staging.txt).
- Request air support early - the head is in heavy/crown fuel; give the aircraft incident coordinates, wind, and the described anchor (05_dispatch_staging.txt).

Crew safety
- LCES confirmed before engagement, with a safety zone sized larger for tall fuels and high wind; escape routes timed under load and re-timed as conditions change (04_lces_safety.txt).
- Mandatory re-brief / disengage triggers: wind shift over ~30 deg, step up in wind, spotting across the line, loss of a lookout or comms (04_lces_safety.txt).

Size-up note
- This incident meets multiple escalation criteria at once - sustained wind above ~25 km/h, fire in crown fuels, high dry fuel: escalate to a larger response immediately and expect the head to outpace crews, so plan from the flanks (01_initial_size_up.txt).

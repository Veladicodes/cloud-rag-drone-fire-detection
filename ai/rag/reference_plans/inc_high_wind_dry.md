Evacuation
- Establish a downwind evacuation arc centred on the incident, oriented along bearing 210 deg (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).
- Base radius ~1.5 km plus ~1 km per 20 km/h of wind: at 26 km/h that is about 2.8 km downwind (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).
- Prioritise notifying occupied structures, campgrounds and single-road communities inside the arc, then routes fire could cross (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).
- Re-issue the zone on any wind shift greater than ~30 deg or a step up in wind speed (03_evacuation_zoning.txt).

Containment
- Begin from an anchor point (road, stream, rock, or old burn) and work the flanks toward the head; do not build line at the head under active spread (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).
- High fuel dryness plus wind above ~20 km/h: use the widest line resources allow, back it with a burnout where safe, and post holding crews (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).
- Keep one flank tied to good black or a firm barrier at all times (02_anchor_and_flank.txt).

Dispatch / staging
- Stage upwind of the incident on a through road that will not be cut, roughly 1 km upwind; move staging if the wind sector shifts toward it (05_dispatch_staging.txt).
- First resource to the anchor point plus a lookout to a vantage; stage further resources rather than committing them until size-up confirms holding points (05_dispatch_staging.txt).
- Request air support early given high fuel dryness and wind; pass incident coordinates, wind, and the described anchor (05_dispatch_staging.txt).

Crew safety
- Confirm LCES before anyone engages: lookouts with a clear view, a confirmed radio link with a check-call schedule, at least two timed escape routes, and a safety zone sized to the fuel and wind (04_lces_safety.txt).
- Re-brief the whole crew on a wind shift over ~30 deg, a step up in wind speed, spotting across the line, or loss of a lookout or comms; when in doubt, disengage to the safety zone (04_lces_safety.txt).

Size-up note
- Record location, current size, fuel type carrying the fire, wind at the incident, and values at risk downwind/downslope within the first minutes (01_initial_size_up.txt).
- Sustained wind above ~25 km/h and high dry fuel meet the escalation criteria — request a larger response now (01_initial_size_up.txt).
